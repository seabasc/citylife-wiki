Community Rules

This section goes for everything CityLife related, whether it is in the Discord, or in-game.

Prohibited Behavior: Harassment, Bullying, Toxicity, Threats, and Privacy Violations
Engaging in any form of harassment, bullying, or toxic behavior-whether inside or outside the server-is
strictly prohibited and will result in disciplinary action. This includes but is not limited to intentionally
provoking or griefing other players, making malicious comments, or showing disrespect toward staff or
fellow community members. Furthermore, any threats made against the community or its members,
including doxing, DDoSing, or releasing personal information, are forbidden. Violations of this rule may
lead to severe consequences, including possible involvement of law enforcement. We enforce a
zero-tolerance policy to maintain a safe, respectful, and enjoyable environment for everyone.

Staff Impersonation
Changing your name or Steam or Discord name to falsely represent yourself as a staff member, as well as
claiming to be staff or impersonating staff in any way, is strictly prohibited.

Age requirement
CityLife is strictly an 18+ server, following the ESRB ratings. Players must be 18 years old or older to play
on the server.

Dishonesty to Staff
Players must be truthful when reporting events to staff. Deliberate falsehoods or misleading information
will result in more severe consequences.

Encouraging Players to break the rules
Getting someone else to do your dirty work, doesn't exclude you from getting in trouble, all parties
involved whether directly, or suggesting will be punished for a rule violation.

General Rules

Working microphone
Players must have a functional, good-quality microphone set to push-to-talk mode to participate in this
server. Failure to meet this requirement may result in removal from the server at the discretion of the
staff.

No Real World Trading (RWT):
Real World Trading (RWT), including buying or selling in-game items, currency, or services for real
currency or external goods, is strictly prohibited. Violation of this rule will result in immediate disciplinary
action, up to and including permanent removal from the server.

Bug Abuse or Exploiting Mechanics:
Exploiting game bugs or abusing mechanics to gain an unfair advantage is strictly prohibited. Examples
of prohibited behavior include:
Using animations to conceal weapons.
Exploiting glitches to move while overweight.
Emoting through walls.
Players found engaging in such behavior will face disciplinary action, ranging from warnings to
permanent bans, depending on the severity and frequency of the offense.

Combat Logging
Combat logging, disconnecting from the server during or immediately after a hostile situation to avoid
roleplay consequences, is strictly prohibited. Players must remain connected and actively participate in all
roleplay interactions.

This rule ensures that players remain engaged in roleplay scenarios and discourages actions that disrupt
immersion and fairness within the CityLife roleplay server. Anyone found doing this will be subject to
punishment.

Scamming
Players are prohibited from scamming others for the following items:
vehicles
Properties.

Third Party Communications
You are required to use the in-game voice chat and radio system at all times when doing anything on the
server. Using 3rd Party Software such as Discord, Teamspeak, Skype, Facetime, etc. shall be considered
meta-gaming.

Blacklisted Modifications
Use of any of the following modifications or similar to your game is extremely prohibited.
. Tracers/Bullet Indicators
. Blood Splatter Effects
. External Crosshairs
. Anything that removes bushes/trees/water/props/weather
. Playing with a widened aspect ratio like 4:3, etc
The only mods allowed are ones that enhance your game, not to give you a competitive edge. ReShade,
ENB, and NVE are okay to use. QuantV isn't due to puremode.

Green Zones
No criminal activity inside or outside hospitals. Do not attempt to use any hospitals as "Safe Zone" to
escape ongoing scenes, you will still be expected to continue the scene there. Do not initiate/provoke at
hospitals, if you start a fight and get killed, that's on you. Do not start any criminal activity at police
stations. Don't attempt any hostage negotiations at police stations, you will get shot.

Streaming
You are not allowed to use the information gained from watching a stream for your in-game character.
This is considered metagaming and will result in a ban. You cannot watch streams while in the server.

Clips/Body cam/Recording
Using recordings to bypass New Life Rule (NLR) or for revenge after death is prohibited. Recordings are
valid only with visible in-game recording devices, like phones or cameras. Businesses may use recordings
as CCTV within their premises, only employees have CCTV. Civilians do not have dash or body cams.

Claims for lost items require video evidence; refunds without proof will be denied. Only Police and EMS
are permitted body cams.

Going AFK
Whilst in-game if anything that happens while you are gone is your own responsibility. If you come
across an AFK player do not kill, harm, rob or mess with them.
AFK farming is not allowed. You cannot be doing jobs/hobbies in the city and go afk. IE. AFK fishing, AFK
mining, etc.

Account Responsibility/Household
Your account, your responsibility. If anything happens even if it is not from you on your account, you will
be punished for it, keep your account private and do not share it with anyone. If you have a family
member or friend playing in the same household, their bans carry onto you. If your brother/sister gets
banned, by default you will also be banned.

Playing Whilst Intoxicated
If you are intoxicated to the point where your ability to follow the rules is impaired, staff have the right to
remove you from the server until you become sober. Furthermore, any rule breaks whilst intoxicated will
be held against you as if you were sober. Drink/smoke and play responsibly.

Character Rules

Before making your character there is important information you should know before doing so!

Realistic Character Names
You must choose a realistic name for your character, it cannot contain any harmful, vulgar language, or
sound like some, and also must not be in connection to any person that would be deemed famous in real
life

Examples:
Do not have names like: "Ben Dover", "Steve Harvey", "Looney Tunes/Chicken Little". Any fictional cartoon
character names/series are not allowed. You will be banned. If this happens to you, open a ticket.

Character Separation and Affiliation
To maintain an authentic and immersive roleplaying environment, each of your characters must exist
independently from one another. They should not share knowledge of personal information,
relationships, or backgrounds beyond what is established in roleplay. Furthermore, having multiple
characters affiliated with the same circle or gang is strictly prohibited. Each character should possess a
unique identity, set of connections, and associations. By respecting this clear separation, you help foster
diverse, engaging, and realistic interactions for everyone in the community.

Wearing Starter Clothing
You must customize your character's appearance upon joining the server for the first time. You cannot
exit the spawn point with the default appearance unless you are heading to the clothing store/barber
shop to fix your look.

Value of liƒe
Your character's life is the most important thing to you, you should not take it's life for granted or as a
joke, and not fear the consequences of getting seriously injured/killed.

Examples:
- Not putting your hands up when being held up
Pulling out a weapon when one/numerous are already drawn towards you
Jumping off high surfaces in an attempt to escape someone, when it's very obvious your
character will die
- Jumping in front of cars that are moving, standing in front of a car in an attempt to stop it
Don't think you're John Wick

Taking a life must have a good roleplay reason behind it, your primary reason for trying to kill someone
should not be so they don't remember something insignificant.

New Life Rule
. If a player chooses to respawn, they are considered to have no memory of the events leading up to
their incapacitation.
. As such, they may not seek revenge on any individual involved in their death.
. A player may only seek revenge if they were:
. Transported to Pillbox Medical, or
. Received medical treatment from ems /pd
. A witness may inform the player of what occurred to refresh their memory, allowing for
appropriate in-character action.

When dying, you should stay away from whoever killed you and not bring up a prior incident for at least
30 minutes. To go back to the killer/confront them you must be reminded by someone. NLR ties into gang
conflict, if your group gets spun and everyone dies/goes down, none of you will know exactly what
happened or who did it. Refrain from talking about details as to what happened when you get
killed/incapped/downed.

Character Killing
For an execution to occur, there must be:
. High-quality roleplay involved
. Ifyou "perma" your character, it must be deleted.

Once you are Character Killed there is no coming back. You can CK by deleting your character or if dying
from a scene you signify in some way that yes, your character is dead. "/me male is dead", "/me no
pulse", etc. This is also with no exceptions, irreversible.

Roleplay Rules

Misuse of /Me's
The purpose of /me's is to describe actions that can't be shown through emotes or aren't visually obvious.
For example, "male smells of alcohol" or "has a bullet in shoulder" are acceptable uses. Inappropriate use
includes "ABC did it to you!" or spamming /me's. If a /me doesn't immediately reveal something you'd

know, like "thinks about killing [Tom]," you won't know about it, but could observe that the person who
did the /me looks angry or etc. Also, avoid reading /me's out loud through voice chat, except when it's
about another character's appearance, like "female would have a missing arm." If you see "Is Looking
into Your Soul," don't acknowledge it.

It's also expected to cooperate with /me's. If prompted by PD/EMS with a "inspects what wounds person
would have," respond with a truthful /me explaining your injuries, or do the same if your radio is
checked.

Illegal Bragging
Posting excessively on social media about drugs/guns/dead bodies/anything illegal will result in a ban, if
you do it occasionally it's okay, be prepared for the Police to investigate you!

Disclosing Illegal Locations
Trying to purchase or sell: drugs, money, or concealed spot locations is prohibited (you can sell drugs, just
not the locations). These locations are intended to be uncovered through roleplay and character
development. Handing out such information too easily undermines the roleplay experience.

Low Effort RP
You should invest effort in your roleplay. Repeatedly shooting, evading the police unnecessarily, and using
extreme actions in minor situations is considered low-effort roleplay. For instance, fleeing a traffic stop
and shooting when you'd only receive a speeding ticket or consistently using motorcycles for robberies.
Always winning every situation is not allowed. Engaging in meaningless shootouts without proper
context or disrupting businesses for enjoyment is unacceptable. Going to Pillbox to hide after committing
serious crimes for 45 minutes to cool off falls into the same category.

Voice ID
If someone has a mask on and in an outfit you've never seen before, you cannot assume who they are
based off of their voice.

Breaking Character
You are expected to stay in character at all times while playing in the server. You should also learn lingo
to help circumvent saying things like "keyboard", "monitor", "pc", etc that refer to things in the real
world.

Examples of breaking character:
· Referring to staff as "gods", "government", etc.
. Referencing your other character as your "cousin"
. Referencing that you record/stream or use the term "bodycam"
. Referencing bans as "deported" or "sent on a vacation"
. Saying "in my head" then proceed to mention something about you IRL.

Body Camping
After killing someone, you cannot stay around the area to ensure EMS are unable to do their job and save
them. If you kill an officer you should not be hanging around the body as multiple officers are going to
arrive and your main focus should be getting out of the area. This isn't exclusive for only prohibiting
PD/EMS, but also stopping other crims/civs trying to help incapped players.

Cop Baiting
Doing stupid things so the police come to you or try to arrest/pull you over (Baiting them to come to
you for a reaction). Absolutely NOT allowed, it will result in a BAN. Also doing anything that would be
deemed completely unrealistic/foolish solely to get a cop's attention. Example: Purposely doing a
burnout/donut in front of a cop at an intersection, near missing a cop on a scene, etc.
(this includes injecting yourself in and around cop scenes if you are not involved)

Vigilante RP
This is where a player takes the law into their own hands rather than calling law enforcement.

Terrorism RP
This is not allowed. However, explosives are available and if RP'd correctly, are allowed.

Inappropriate Sexual Conduct
If you want to indulge in erotic roleplay, both parties must give consent. If both parties do not consent
then this would be considered sexual harassment which is taken extremely seriously. You will be banned
without question and your appeal will not be looked at.

On the topic of sexual discourse, the unwanted comments about another player's sexual anatomy or
genitalia are considered inappropriate. The discussion of sexual themes needs consent of all parties
involved.

No Intent to Roleplay
Exactly as it sounds, if you do not have any intent to actually roleplay here you will be removed.

Fail RP
Fail RP refers to unrealistic actions or a failure to roleplay appropriately. This term covers a wide range of
actions, all of which are prohibited. For instance, passing items through 5-inch thick glass to a friend in
jail

Bringing individuals to Grandma's/the Hospital to revive them and then robbing, torturing, or killing
them is grounds for a ban. Once you've killed someone for a specific reason, you cannot harm or pursue
them further for the same reason; it's over, and you should keep your distance.

Metagaming:
Using any information that your character did not obtain through roleplay to affect in-game actions or
decisions. This includes using information from discord or live streams. Mixing memories from 2 different
characters is not allowed. If you are restrained or if your communication devices have been removed then
you must make yourself inactive on any other forms of communication besides proximity chat.

Powergaming
Trying to misuse roleplay to exploit game mechanics for an advantage or winning within roleplay. Such as
excessive jump spamming, failing to correctly assess your injuries, or using radio unrealistically when
handcuffed or with hands up, is not allowed. Quick example: hiding equipped weapons via emotes.

Using combat helmets as a civilian or criminal is also prohibited. Using bulletproof louvers on the back of
your vehicle is not allowed. Installing bulletproof body panels/windows is not allowed.

Abuse of a Whitelisted Job
You should not be conducting any illegal activities while on duty at your job. You should not be on duty
and not do your job, just to receive a paycheck.

Addition to this, you cannot abuse in-game mechanics granted by your job to benefit your experience,
examples are:
. Mechanics putting parts on their own cars for free
. Dynasty Agents giving themselves a free house
. Un-jailing/un-bedding your friends from the hospital because they're "your friend"
. Using any discord log or channel information to gain in-character information (eg: license plates)

When joining a whitelisted job, your intention should be to perform your duties fairly and in line with the
job's objectives. This means:
. Cops should not leave evidence on their friends, give their friends unfair advantages, sell
evidence, improperly transfer evidence, be corrupt, etc.
. DOJ members should not utilize the MDT for personal benefit outside of lawyer duties, same with
EMS, etc.

Group Limits
You shall not be in a group that is larger than 5 people in criminal scenes. This number will not change
regardless of police presence. Swapping members out mid-scene is strictly prohibited. Lookouts, drivers,
and scouts count towards the number. To put it plainly, the only people involved at all should be the 5
chosen. Nobody outside the 5 should be aiding in any way to the scene. They shouldn't be on the radio
giving you callouts, preparing cars, picking up bodies before the scene is over, absolutely nothing. It's up
to that 5 and that's it.

You can not Impersonate Officers/EMS/DoJ
Do not use the clothing/ped models that are associated with emergency services.
Stealing emergency vehicles without any RP involved falls under this category. This includes ANY plane(s),
helicopters, tanks, jets, transport vehicles, cargobobs, military helicopters etc as well. It is also frowned
upon to attempt to scam players of services that judges would perform, like selling gun licenses, name
changes, expungements, etc.

Gang Conflict
In the sense of griefing, "beef" between two groups must have valid roleplay reasoning behind it, so if a
third party is told about it, they could also agree as to why there is beef in the first place. It must make
sense, and have high quality roleplay behind it. Beef shouldn't be forced upon another group, because
clearly if it's valid, both sides would understand it. During the beef, both parties must be providing high
quality roleplay the entire time, at no point should anyone be delivering anything but that, or breaking
rules. Group managers/staff have the right to step in and forcefully stop/alter beef at any time if deemed
necessary.

Ocean Dumping
Ocean dumping should be a last resort and used sparingly.

When someone is ocean dumped, they forget major storyline details related to a group or activity they
should forget. Those initiating the ocean dump must explain why they're doing it, like, "We can't let you
roam freely knowing our operations."

Valid reasons for ocean dumping include:
- A cop discovering your gang's plans or illegal hideout, which, if remembered, could lead to
trouble like raids.
Someone is leaving your gang, and you want them to forget critical information related to it.
Catching someone repeatedly spying on your gang, and they know too much.

Be aware that improperly or invalidly dumping someone results in harsh punishments, regardless of your
ban history. If an ocean dump is deemed invalid, the victim player(s) will be immune from further
attempts and they'll retain all storyline information they were meant to forget.

Situational Rules

Getting Downed
You must tell the truth about what happened (Stabbed, Gunshots, Runover) if you don't this will be
deemed as Fail RP. You can talk when you're down as long as the injury isn't immediately fatal, and when
you talk it's about your injuries. so feel free to RP groaning, crying, etc. You should not be talking
normally if you're downed/dead. Talking trash to a dead body excessively is not tolerated.

Combat Rules

Random Death Match (RDM)
Attacking another player/group without any story/roleplay leading up to it. There should always be build
up or a story behind every death. You must initiate prior to shooting/killing someone. Reference "Value of
Life," what happens in the scene must be valid for the attempt/murder of the other player. You should not
kill someone for being in a certain area when the job forces them to be there (legal jobs like trucking,
fishing, hunting, etc.). With initiation, it should not be a one-line thing; there should actively be back &
forth dialogue, RP is a two-way street. The only exception to this is at Airdrops or Outpost.

Examples of bad reasons/initiations:
. Any "one liners," "what those colors mean to you," "hey buddy," "stay off our block!," "abc did it
to you!”
. Killing someone because they are annoying
. Killing someone because you got into a vehicle accident with them
. Killing someone because they are refusing you service at businesses/courthouse
. Killing someone because they are doing a legal job (trucking, fishing, hunting, etc.)
. Killing someone because they are wearing a random piece of clothing that has the same color as
your gang
. Killing someone because their vehicle is the color of your gang. You will be permanently banned
ƒor this.

Examples of good reasons/initiations:
. Escalated tension from multiple disrespectful encounters (not just a single comment)
. A known rival gang entering your turf during an active beef and provoking your group
Someone snitching on your gang with clear IC proof (bodycam, wire, etc.)
. Retaliating for a recent shooting, robbery, or ambush by a rival gang

. A player actively interfering with your illegal operation (trap spot, drop, etc.)
You're threatened with serious harm (robbery, death) and follow up later with planned retaliation
· A kidnapping setup used to intimidate or provoke a rival gang
. A rival consistently provoking conflict across multiple scenes with clear RP buildup

If a shootout pauses for over 5 minutes (both parties leave the scene), you must reinitiate.

Vehicular Death Match (VDM)
Attacking another player/group using a vehicle, the attempt is still not allowed.

GTA Driving
Driving a vehicle in a manner that any sensible person would deem as incredibly unrealistic as it'd cause
severe injury or death to you and others. You can take stunt jumps around the map, especially in getaway
chases. Some examples of what not to do are:
. Do not drive off the pier or into the ocean
. Do not drive into vehicles head-on, as a heat seeking missile
. Do not drive a vehicle off-road/up a mountain, that clearly shouldn't be
. Do not ram other vehicles at speeds that would cause severe injury or death
. Do not use your vehicle to purposely disable other vehicles
. Do not drive off a very high point in a motorcycle/car

Hostages/Kidnapping
When taking hostages, you must provide high-quality roleplay. You can not use fake hostages such as
friends or group members. Hostages should be kept no longer than 45 minutes if a scene is not lasting
longer. Generally, the police only allow 1 demand per hostage. If you wish to kidnap a member of PD or
EMS, it must be for a valid RP reason that ties directly into the storyline.

If members are being chased by the cops they are not allowed to go back to your block where there are
an excess of 5 members to set up an ambush.

If another group rolls up to your street and opens fire, you are only allowed to chase with a maximum of
5 people no exceptions.

Gang Wars
A Gang War is a war gangs agree to go into against each other where they can set terms and
stipulations. Official Gang Wars must have terms agreed to and approved by a Group Manager (staff).
These terms can alter some of the basic Combat and Criminal rules of the server.

Recording Gameplay
. Always Record: You must be recording your gameplay at all times, including both game and mic
audio.
. Retention: Keep all clips for at least 7 days after any incident.

. Staff Requests: If staff requests your footage (for kills or other incidents) and you cannot provide
it, you will be banned for 2 weeks.
. No Editing/Tampering: Edited or incomplete clips aren't allowed. Tampering or providing fake
evidence results in permanent removal.
. Recommended Software: We suggest using NVIDIA Shadowplay or Medal.tv.

Robbery Rules

Lootboxing
Looting any player without much roleplay presented for them is prohibited.

Robbing stores/banks
You can not chain rob stores or banks, please use common sense. You must wait a minimum of 45
minutes between store robberies and minimum 2 hours between bank robberies.
. You can not use super/hyper cars in store/bank robberies.
. Do not initiate any heists 15 minutes prior/post server restart
. You cannot counter a heist/store robbery that is already in progress.

Robbing
Robbing should not be motivated by financial gain; avoid robbing people simply because you're low on
ƒunds.

To initiate a robbery, two essential conditions must be met:
. High-quality roleplay is required.
. A compelling reason for the robbery must be established.

During a robbery, you must continue to engage in dialogue after initiating the hold-up. Remaining silent
during a robbery will result in a ban. Ideally, while the victim is being held up, someone in your group
should explain why they're being robbed. For example, "You had it coming; shouldn't have kept coming
back after we told you this isn't your spot"effort

When robbing someone, you can only take what's in their inventory. You cannot demand bank transfers,
force them to sell their cars, or remove items from their home. Excessive robbing and inventory wiping
are not allowed, meaning don't wipe it or take random items that don't make sense.

Chain robbing at an illegal location without a strong roleplay background is strictly prohibited.
Consistently robbing every player you encounter is not allowed.

This rule is heavily enforced. Please know your limits before attempting to rob an officer. Failure to abide
by this will result in harsh punishments of 2 weeks to a month ban. Robbing law enforcement should not
be for financial gain or due to them performing their duty. It has to be when an officer has gone above
and beyond their line of duty in some way with criminals, trash talking or being called mean names
doesn't warrant a cop being robbed. It must involve proper premeditated roleplay, and be approved by
an admin+.

You cannot rob or kidnap the same player more than once in a 2-hour period. If you rob someone, you
can't later kill them if they comply; it's one or the other. You either kill them or rob them and let them go.

If you're being robbed, refrain from taunting or trash-talking the robbers, thinking you're immune to
being killed. Play the victim role.

Outside Help
If you involve anyone outside your immediate group during a heist (such as store or bank robberies), you
must inform the police at the scene. Having a getaway driver or a non-violent accomplice waiting outside
is allowed, but you may not organize a planned ambush to kill police. Any outside assistance should
remain passive and not act as gunmen waiting to engage without initiation. If the situation escalates,
using deadly force with outside help should be a last resort-only after all other high-effort escape
options have failed. Planning a violent intervention from the start is considered low-effort roleplay and
will result in a ban for the entire group.

CityLife Gang Guidelines

Groups seeking whitelisted faction status must meet the following criteria:

. Demonstrate an active presence within the server and gain community recognition.
. Exhibit strong roleplay skills that align with server rules as a group.
. Have between 10 and 25 members.
. Maintain a commitment to authentic criminal gang/organization roleplay.
. Have been part of the server for at least 30 days.
. Develop depth around your group to ensure it is ready for official gang recognition.

Note: The whitelisting process applies only to criminal roleplay groups. Family
or friend groups should not apply.

INACTIVITY & STRIKES

. Inactive gangs may lose access to their bench or warehouse.
. Rule-breaking involving multiple members may result in a gang strike.
. Gang strikes last for 60 days before being removed.
. Gang leaders are responsible for their members' actions.

BLOCK RULES

. No Block Baiting: Do not bait near a block to gain an advantage.
. Block Pushing: After winning a 5-man fight, gangs may push an opposing gang's
block immediately.
. A 30-minute cooldown begins once a block push is initiated.

Whitelisted vs. Non-Whitelisted Gangs:

. Non-whitelisted gangs cannot contest the block of a whitelisted gang.
. Non-whitelisted gangs may use and defend an unclaimed block, but they cannot
claim it until whitelisted.

Block Antagonism:

. Circling another gang's block without intent to engage will be considered baiting.

Castle Rule
The castle rule is when players in a gang and/or group are hanging out PASSIVELY
at their known OWNED street/block/area while over group limit. If another player
or group attempts to shoot at the players that are already passively hanging out
they may return fire, in self defense.
. This only applies for whitelisted gangs, if you are not whitelisted you cannot
utilize any "castle" to your advantage.
