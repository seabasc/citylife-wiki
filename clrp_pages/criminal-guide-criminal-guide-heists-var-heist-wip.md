# VAR Heist (WIP)

 The VAR Heist is fully functional in-city, the Wiki just needs updating!

1. **Confirm Participant Requirements:** Confirm that participant requirements are met before beginning.
2. **Prepare Required Equipment:** Bring the required equipment and use it in accordance with the applicable rules.
3. **Officer Roll Call:** Confirm that the required number of officers are on duty before starting.
4. **Complete Required Actions:** Disable the power at the Electrical Box. Complete the required vault, deposit-box, and barrier interactions with the required tools.
5. **Collect the Available Items:** Collect the available vault items, including trolleys and tables, then prepare to leave.
6. **Make Your Escape:** Leave the area using a planned route.

   Follow all applicable rules throughout the activity.
