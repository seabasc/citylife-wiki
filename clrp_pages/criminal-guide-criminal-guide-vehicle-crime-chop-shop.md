# Chop Shop

 Chop Shop, the guide to scrapping cars the illegal way!

> **Note:** Whence you start the job, look out for a blue circle on your map!

1. Prepare the required equipment. First up, secure yourself a super-sleek VPN.
2. Once you've got that in your toolkit, open your phone and launch the Job Center app and press on Chop shop with a tap.&#x20;
3. Once the requirements are met, begin the activity. Use the relevant in-game application to follow the objective. Review the requirements before starting.
4. Depending on your level, you will unlock different car models, locations, and better rewards!

> **Note:** See the linked Chop Shop article for unlock details!
