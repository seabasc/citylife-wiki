# Maintenance & Repairs

Your components wear down over time while actively mining. Keep an eye on their health!

> **Note:** : Higher tier parts last longer AND can be repaired more times. Finding that hidden workshop and investing in upgrades saves money long term!

### Health System

Parts degrade based on active mining time (not real time). When any part drops below 25% health, your miner stops working.

### Repair Kits

| Repair Kit                     | Tiers it Repairs      | How to Get                                       | Price                |
| ------------------------------ | --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ | -------------------- |
| Basic Electronic Repair Kit    | Tier 1 parts only     | Purchasable at Digital Den                       | $1,150               |
| Advanced Electronic Repair Kit | Tier 2 & Tier 3 parts | Craftable at Electronics Table (hidden location) | \~$1,150 (materials) |

### Repair Limits:

* Tier 1 parts: Can be repaired 2 times before replacement
* Tier 2 parts: Can be repaired 3 times before replacement&#x20;
* Tier 3 parts: Can be repaired 4 times before replacement

> **Note:** : Repairing a part does NOT reset its age! Old parts that have been mining for weeks will degrade quickly again after repair.
