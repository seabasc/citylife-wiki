# Crypto Mining

This job is a bit different, and focuses on passive income through Crypto Mining! Build your own mining rig, upgrade your components, and watch your crypto wallet grow!

> **Note:** You need level 5 Crafting to craft your first Crypto Miner!

## How do you start Crypto Mining?

### To start Crypto Mining, Visit The Digital Den on your GPS!&#x20;

<figure><img src="assets/wiki/4822a83a0db30ddae463.png" alt="" width="247"><figcaption><p>Visit the Digital Den on your GPS!</p></figcaption></figure>

1. **Visit Digital Den:** Head over to the Digital Den electronics store on your GPS! This is your one-stop shop for all things tech-related, and home to the crafting bench where you'll build your first mining rig.
2. **Stock up**: Now, purchase all the parts you'll need to build your first Mining Rig. You'll need a Tier 1 Motherboard, GPU, RAM, Cooling System, Power Supply, Storage Drive, and a Computer Case!
3. Craft Your Starter Rig: Use the crafting bench and your freshly purchased parts at Digital Den to build your first rig (You will need Crafting Level 5)
4. **Place Your Miner Indoors**: Your crypto miner must be placed inside a property you own or have access to. Head into your house or warehouse, use the miner from your inventory, and position it where you'd like. Warehouses can hold up to 4 miners if you're looking to scale up your operation!
5. **Select Your Crypto**: Interact with your placed miner to choose which cryptocurrency to mine. Start with Shungite - it's perfect for beginners. As you upgrade your hardware, you'll unlock X Coin and the premium LME Coin.
6. The Waiting Game Begins: Patience is key in crypto mining! Your rig will keep crunching numbers and generating cryptocurrency without any extra effort from you. Just sit back, relax, and let the passive income roll in. Mining updates happen every 20 minutes.
7. Reap the Benefits: Once your miner has accumulated crypto, interact with it to withdraw directly to your phone's crypto wallet. Watch out for storage limits though - if your miner fills up, it stops mining until you withdraw!
