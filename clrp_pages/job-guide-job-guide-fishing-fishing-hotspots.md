# Fishing Hotspots

> **Note:** Fishing Hotspots rotate every hour!

## Hotspot Information

* Hotspots **rotate every 60 minutes** to different ocean, lake, and Alamo Sea locations
* You'll receive a **notification** when the hotspot moves if you're inside it or have hotspot notifications enabled
* The hotspot is marked on the map with a **blip** (only visible if you own a fishing rod or use `/showhotspots`

## Hotspot Bonus's

* **Better Catch Rate** — 15% increased chance to successfully catch fish&#x20;
* **Faster Fishing** — 15% reduction in wait times between casts
* **More Experience** — 20% bonus XP for each catch&#x20;
