# Woodcutting

Woodcutting is an easy-to-learn skill that levels up by chopping down Trees! Below is your Woodcutting skill cheat-sheet.

## Unlockable

* [x] Reaching Level 5 Unlocks Cutting Oak Trees!
* [x] Reaching Level 10 Unlocks the Steel Axe & Cutting Maple Trees!
* [x] Reaching Level 15 Unlocks Cutting Willow Trees!
* [x] Reaching Level 20 Unlocks the Copper Axe & Cutting Birch Trees!
* [x] Reaching Level 25 Unlocks Cutting Pine Trees!
* [x] Reaching Level 30 Unlocks the Silver Axe & Cutting Hickory Trees!
* [x] Reaching Level 40 Unlocks Cutting Cedar Trees!
* [x] Reaching Level 45 Unlocks the Gold Axe!
* [x] Reaching Level 50 Unlocks Cutting Chestnut Trees!
* [x] Reaching Level 60 Unlocks the Diamond Axe & Cutting Redwood Trees!
* [x] Reaching Level 70 Unlocks Cutting Agarwood Trees!
* [x] Reaching Level 80 Unlocks Cutting Boxwood & Baobab Trees!
