# Pawnshop Rings

Craft powerful magical rings at the Pawnshop to gain unique passive buffs! Each ring provides a different advantage but loses durability over time.

### What You'll Need

* Find a Pawnshop employee when the shop is open
* Bring Celestial Alloy - The rare material required for ALL rings
* Bring the required gems and materials

### The Rings

| Ring            | Effect                                                        | Durability Loss  |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------- |
| Ruby Ring       | Regenerates 2 HP every 15 seconds                             | 0.5% per minute  |
| Sapphire Ring   | Regenerates armor to 33% (2 armor/15s)                        | 0.5% per minute  |
| Emerald Ring    | Restores stamina when sprinting (5 uses, then 1 min cooldown) | 0.5% per minute  |
| Citrine Ring    | Removes 2 stress every 15 seconds                             | 0.5% per minute  |
| Aquamarine Ring | Faster swimming speed                                         | 0.5% per minute  |
| Onyx Ring       | Way slower hunger and thirst drain                            | 0.25% per minute |

### Usage & Maintenance

* Activation: Costs 2% durability instantly
* Passive Drain: Rings lose durability while active
* Repair: Use a Ring Repair Kit at the Pawnshop repair bench (requires Pawnshop employee)
* Cooldown: 5 seconds between equipping different rings
* One at a Time: Only one ring buff can be active

### Pro Tips

* Onyx Ring is the most efficient (0.25% drain vs 0.5%)
* Ruby & Sapphire great for combat survivability
* Emerald perfect for runners and job activities
* Aquamarine essential for diving activities
* Monitor durability - rings break at 0% and need repair!
