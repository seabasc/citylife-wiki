 Growing and Selling Procedure

> **Note:** Want to learn more about the Skill unlocks for Drug Sales? Click [HERE](/skill-guide/skill-guide/drug-sales.md)

1. **Gather Your Gardening Gear**: Embark on your botanical journey by collecting essential gardening supplies from Smoke on the Water. You'll need plant water, plant fertilizer, and plant pots!

2. **Northbound for Nurturing Nature**: Adventure northward to the renowned Weed Farm! Here's where you'll lovingly pick weed plants for those precious seeds. Keep an eye out for OG Kush seeds, as this is where your growing begins!
3. **Seeds to Success**: With your seed collection complete, find a tranquil spot that's off the beaten path. It's time to plant your seeds and start your secret garden!
4. **TLC for Your Plants**: Monitor your plants like a proud plant parent (Third eye). Proper watering and feeding are crucial for top-notch quality — remember, it's all about the end product!
5. **Harvest, Roll, and Entrepreneurship**: Now comes the thrilling conclusion! Harvest your plants, roll those joints at a covert location, and then engage in the fine art of salesmanship with locals using our innovative corner selling script (activated through the Z radial menu).
