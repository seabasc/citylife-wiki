# Growing and Selling

 Growing and Selling Guide

> **Note:** Want to learn more about the Skill unlocks for Drug Sales? Click [HERE](/skill-guide/skill-guide/drug-sales.md)

1. **Gather Your Gardening Gear**: Collect plant water, plant fertilizer, and plant pots from Smoke on the Water.

2. **Northbound for Nurturing Nature**: Travel north to the Weed Farm and pick weed plants to obtain OG Kush seeds.
3. **Seeds to Success**: With your seed collection complete, find a tranquil spot that's off the beaten path. It's time to plant your seeds and start your secret garden!
4. **TLC for Your Plants**: Monitor the plants using the Third eye. Water and fertilize them properly to maintain product quality.
5. **Harvest, Roll, and Entrepreneurship**: Harvest the plants, roll the joints at a covert location, and sell them to locals using the corner selling script in the Z radial menu.

***

Embrace your inner botanist and remember, this guide is for virtual green thumbs only.
