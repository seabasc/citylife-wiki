# Mushrooms

The Forager's Guide: Mastering the Art of Harvesting and Processing Mushrooms

> **Note:** Eating a mushroom may just prove useful in finding more!

1. **Into the Wild!** Head to the forest spot shown below and take a leisurely stroll along the paths – keep an eye out for fungi friends along the way.
2. **Mushroom Mania!** When you spot a mushroom, don’t just stare – harvest it like the pro forager you are! (LALT)
3. **Mix It Up!** Locate the processing site and get ready for some mushroom alchemy. Oh, and there’s a secret ingredient you'll need – but no spoilers here! Time to flex those RP skills and uncover the mystery. Good luck, shroom whisperer!
