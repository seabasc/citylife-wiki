# How to Get Whitelisted

Becoming an official whitelisted gang unlocks exclusive features like territory, gang sprays, crafting benches, and more. But whitelist status isn't handed out freely, it's earned.

### Groups seeking whitelisted faction status must:

| Requirement          | Details                                                                |
| -------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Established Presence | Demonstrate a presence within the server and gain recognition          |
| Strong Roleplay      | Exhibit strong roleplay skills that align with server rules as a group |
| Member Count         | Have no more than 25 members and no fewer than 5 members               |
| Authentic RP         | Maintain a commitment to authentic criminal gang/organization roleplay |

***

### How to Apply

Once your gang meets the requirements above, submit a whitelist application through our official Discord: CityLife Gangs & Factions Discord

***

### What Happens Next?

* Staff will review your gang's activity, reputation, and history
* You may be asked questions or given feedback
* If approved, your gang will be granted whitelist status and gain access to all exclusive features

***

> **Note:** Whitelist status can be revoked if your gang fails to maintain good standing or breaks server rules. There are also strike systems in place!
