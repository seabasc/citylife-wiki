# Whitelisted Gangs

 This page describes the available unlocks you obtain when your gang is officially Whitelisted!!

#### Official Territory

Whitelisted gangs receive designated territory on the map.. This area becomes your gang's turf where you can establish dominance, conduct operations, and defend against rivals.

#### Gang Sprays & Graffiti

* Claim Territory - Spray your gang's custom graffiti on walls to mark turf
* Turf Radius - Each spray claims a radius around it
* NPC Dealers - Gang NPCs spawn near your sprays
* Contest Rival Sprays - Scratch enemy graffiti to take over their territory
* Spray Shop - Purchase spray cans (price scales with level) and spray remover

#### Crafting Bench

Each whitelisted gang receives a dedicated crafting bench at a unique location. At the bench, gang members can craft:

* Weapons
* Ammunition
* Armor
* Gang-exclusive items
* Other crafting recipes

#### Rob Law Enforcement

Whitelisted gangs gain access to the ability to rob police officers. This high risk activity allows gang members to take items from law enforcement during confrontations. Whitelisted gangs are trusted to not abuse this ability.
