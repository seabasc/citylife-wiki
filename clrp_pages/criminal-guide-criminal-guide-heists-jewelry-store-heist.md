# Jewelry Store Heist

 Jewelry Store Heist Guide

1. **Recruit a Hostage (or two)**: Recruit one or two local hostages through persuasion. This is required before proceeding.
2. **Gather Your Tools of the Trade**: Before you can make your grand entrance at Vangelico, you'll need to arm yourself with the essentials: a trusty gun for persuasion, a knife for those tricky paintings, and a plasma cutter for the main event.
3. **Check the Officer Count**: Ensure there are at least four brave officers of the law present in the city. It’s only fair to give them a sporting chance, right?
4. **Locate a Master Technician**: Now, find yourself a genius of the electrical arts. You'll need their skills to conveniently disrupt and cut into the power supply right in front of Vangelico. Timing is everything!
5. **Execute Your Master Plan Inside**: With the lights out, it's time to shine! Proceed inside with your well-prepared team and let the heist unfold as you've planned.
6. **Complete the Getaway**: Complete the heist by executing the planned getaway.
