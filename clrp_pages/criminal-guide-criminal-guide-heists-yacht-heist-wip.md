# Yacht Heist (WIP)

 The Yacht Heist is fully functional in-city, the Wiki just needs updating!

1. **Select a Hostage:** Select a hostage to begin the heist. Charm and persuasion may be used instead of force.
2. **Prepare Equipment:** Bring a weapon and any other equipment required for the heist.
3. **Officer Roll Call:** Confirm that at least four officers are on duty before beginning the heist.
4. **Complete the Heist:** Cut the power at the Electrical Box, then hack the vault door. Drill into the safety deposit boxes and use thermite to cut through the barriers.
5. **Collect Available Items:** Collect all available vault items, including those on the trolleys and tables.
6. **Make Your Escape:** Complete the final step by leaving the area using the planned escape route.

   Follow all applicable rules throughout the heist.
