# House Packages

 Unlocked at Thieving Chapter 35

> **Note:** Residents may be home! Stay cautious and be alert!

1. To steal residential packages, search neighborhoods for houses with a package left on the porch.
2. When you find your target, you must be thieving chapter 35, then simply grab a Lockpick, and third eye (L ALT) the package!
