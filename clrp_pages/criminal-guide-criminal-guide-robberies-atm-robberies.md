# ATM Robberies

 Quick cash is just a drill, or a rope away.

1. **Scout Your Target**   \
   ATMs are scattered throughout the city. Find one in a quiet spot away from prying eyes, or go bold and hit one in broad daylight. Your call, your risk.
2. **Officer Check**   \
   Confirm that the required number of officers are on duty before starting.
3. **Choose Your Method**   \
   There are two ways to crack an ATM, each with its own trade offs:

#### **METHOD 1: The Drill**

Faster execution, standard payout What You'll Need:

* Drill – Your primary breaching tool
* Drill Bit – Can't drill without it

**How It Works**:

1. Approach the ATM and equip your drill
2. Bore through the ATM's security panel
3. Extract the cash once the drilling is complete
4. Get out fast, drilling makes noise...

#### METHOD 2: The Rope

Takes longer, bigger payout. What You'll Need:

* A Vehicle, Something with towing power
* Rope, To yank the ATM from its foundation
* A Jack hammer

How It Works:

1. Secure a vehicle capable of pulling the ATM
2. Attach the rope to both your vehicle and the ATM
3. Floor it and rip the ATM from the ground
4. The extraction takes time, defend your position
5. Once loose, drive around until the ATM Security System De-activates
6. Now that you've lost the heat, it's time to crack into the ATM, the old fashioned way\\
