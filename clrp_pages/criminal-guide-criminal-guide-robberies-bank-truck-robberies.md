# Bank Truck Robberies

Bank Truck Guide

1. **Secure the Insider Tip**\
   Bring the requested special item to the hidden black-market dealer and exchange it for details about the bank truck routes. The dealer will not provide the information without the item.
2. **Officer Check**\
   Confirm that the required number of officers are on duty before beginning.
3. **Mark the Route**\
   Once you’ve got the intel, your map or GPS will reveal the truck’s location. Time is of the essence—bank trucks won’t wait around. Rally your crew and get moving.
4. **Lock and Load**\
   The bank truck is heavily guarded. Bring weapons, ammunition, and any additional equipment needed, such as explosives or thermite.
5. **Ambush the Truck**\
   When you arrive on scene, coordinate your strike:
   * **Eliminate the Guards**: Swiftly take out the escort detail. Hesitate, and they’ll call for backup.
   * **Access the Truck**: Plant or place your explosive device to breach the armored doors. A loud boom might attract attention, so expect reinforcements.
6. **Loot the Haul**\
   You’ve come this far for what’s inside. Snatch up everything you can—cash, valuables, or any special items that might be tucked away. The faster you work, the better your odds of a clean getaway.
7. **The Great Escape**\
   With the truck’s contents in hand, it’s time to vanish. Plan your exit route—get creative with decoys or speed off in a getaway ride. Survival and style are both part of the prize.
