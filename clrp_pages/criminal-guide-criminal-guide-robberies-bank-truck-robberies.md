# Bank Truck Robberies

Bank Truck Procedure

1. **Secure the Insider Tip**\
   Begin by obtaining the required information. In this case, head to the hidden black-market dealer, bringing along a specific “special item” they’re after. Trade that item for exclusive details on the bank truck’s secret routes—no item, no intel.
2. **Officer Check**\
   Confirm that the required number of officers are on duty before starting.
3. **Mark the Route**\
   Once you’ve got the intel, your map or GPS will reveal the truck’s location. Proceed to the marked location promptly. Coordinate with the required participants.
4. **Lock and Load**\
   These aren’t standard delivery drivers; they’re heavily guarded. Come prepared with weapons, ammo, and any extra firepower you might need—explosives, thermite, or whatever suits your style.
5. **Ambush the Truck**\
   When you arrive on scene, coordinate your strike:
   * **Eliminate the Guards**: Swiftly take out the escort detail. Hesitate, and they’ll call for backup.
   * **Access the Truck**: Plant or place your explosive device to breach the armored doors. A loud boom might attract attention, so expect reinforcements.
6. **Loot the Haul**\
   Collect the available items. Snatch up everything you can—cash, valuables, or any special items that might be tucked away. The faster you work, the better your odds of a clean getaway.
7. **Exit Procedure**\
   With the truck’s contents in hand, it’s time to vanish. Plan your exit route—get creative with decoys or speed off in a getaway ride. Survival and style are both part of the prize.
