# House Robberies

House Robberies Procedure

1. **Find the Right Boss**\
   Your first stop is the underworld contact who deals in black-market break-ins. Seek out this “boss” for the chance to prove your skill. Without their nod of approval, you won’t get anywhere near the lucrative neighborhoods.
2. **Choose the Perfect Moment**\
   Patience pays off in any successful robbery. Timing is everything, and you’ll likely need a certain number of officers on duty to keep the heat balanced. If there aren’t enough officers patrolling, you’ll have to lie low until circumstances are right.
3. **Arm Yourself with the Right Tools**\
   Houses aren’t pushovers—especially in a well-protected city. Secure any specialized gear your that's required for a forced entry. If you show up unprepared, you’ll be turning around empty-handed.
4. **Get the Target Location**\
   Once you’re set with your gear, return to the boss. They’ll give you a location that’s ripe for the picking. Listen carefully—the last thing you want to do is break into the wrong place!
5. **Enter with Caution**\
   Slip inside quietly. Keep your noise to a minimum—pets might bark, neighbors might snoop, and homeowners might be lurking. One wrong move can set off alarms, so stay on your toes.
6. **Follow the Group App**\
   If your crew has a shared app or in-game system, keep an eye on it for any updates or intel. New objectives might pop up as you move from room to room, like searching for safes or picking the right locks.
7. **Exit and Evade**\
   Once you’ve gathered the goods, head for the exit. Make it quick—every second spent inside risks discovery. When you’re clear, hope your luck holds out and no sirens catch up to you.
8. **Reap the Rewards (Or Lie Low)**\
   If all goes well, drop off your haul or lay low for a bit. Bragging rights aside, you don’t want your name circulating among the wrong crowds—or the local police blotter.
