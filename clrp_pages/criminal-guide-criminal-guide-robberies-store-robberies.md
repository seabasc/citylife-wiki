# Store Robberies

 Store Robbery Guide

1. **Select a Hostage**: Select a hostage to begin the robbery. Charm and persuasion may be used instead of force.
2. **Arm Yourself Wisely**: Before you set foot in your targeted store, make sure you're equipped with a gun. It's not just a tool; it's the tool required to proceed!
3. **Officer Check-In**: Ensure there are at least two dedicated officers in the city. It’s only fair to have a bit of a challenge, right?
4. **Take Control with Creativity**: Take control of the store by pointing your weapon and proceeding through force or persuasion as appropriate.
5. **Speed is Your Best Friend**: Leave the area as quickly as possible.
