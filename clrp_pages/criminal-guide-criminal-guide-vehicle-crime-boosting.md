# Boosting

Boosting, the guide to obtaining cars the illegal way!

> **Note:** Whence you start the job, look out for a blue circle on your map!

1. Prepare the required equipment. First up, secure yourself a super-sleek VPN and a Laptop from Digital Den!
2. Once you've got that in your toolkit, you'll just need two more items! An ECU Bypasser, and a Signal Jammer (For B Class boosts and above)
3. Once the requirements are met, begin the activity. Your Boosting Laptop & Phone are your guide! Review the requirements before starting.
4. Depending on your Boosting level, you will unlock different car models, locations, and better rewards including the ability to VIN Scratch vehicles!
