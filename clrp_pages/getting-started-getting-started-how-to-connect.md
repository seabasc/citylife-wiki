# How to Connect

> **Note:** : Before connecting, it is HIGHLY recommended that you read the [Rules](wiki:rules)!

1. Please ensure that you have the <mark style="color:blue;">Civilian</mark> rank on our Discordbefore attempting to join!
2. On the FiveM main menu, press **F8.**
3. Type “connect play.cl-rp.ne&#x74;**”** in the text bar and press ENTER
4. Press F8 to close the console and click on **“PLAY NOW”** when the button appears.

<details>

<summary>Having issues connecting?</summary>

1. Do you have the Civilian Rank in our Discord? If not, click the green button in #Rules
2. Try restarting FiveM and Steam if you can't join
3. Try clearing your FiveM Cache
4. Try running Steam as Administrator

</details>
