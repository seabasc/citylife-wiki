# Diving

> **Note:** : Drowning may occur!

## How do you start Diving?

### To start Diving, head over to the Diving blip on your GPS!

<figure><img src="assets/wiki/c834497d3a592cabc1c1.png" alt=""><figcaption><p>POSTAL: 8212</p></figcaption></figure>

1. **Meet the boss:** Make your way to 8212 and engage with Boss Finley using your third eye.
2. **Shop for Equipment:** Open the Scuba Shop through Finley and purchase the essential gear you'll need, including diving refills and diving equipment!
3. **Create a Group:** Utilize the group app to create your team.
4. **Rent a Boat:** Rent your diving vessel directly from Finley.
5. **Diving Gear:** Don't forget to purchase your diving equipment from Finley as well.
6. **Head to the Marker:** Follow the marker on your map to your diving destination.
7. **Suit Up:** Once you've reached your destination, it's time to equip your diving gear.
8. **Collect Crates:** Dive deep into the water and start collecting Finley's crates.
9. **Optional Coral Collection:** While you're down there, keep an eye out for beautiful corals you can gather. (It pays well!)
10. **Monitor Progress:** Keep tabs on your progress using the group app. Be sure to stay alert for an email from Finley containing further instructions.
11. **Follow New Directions:** If Finley points you to a new location, make your way there. If not, return to the shop and wrap up the activity.
12. **Sell Corals:** Any corals you've gathered can be sold back to Finley for a tidy profit.
13. **Need Help?:** If you require more guidance, feel free to have a chat with Finley and select "activity instructions."

> **Note:** See the linked Diving article for unlock details!
