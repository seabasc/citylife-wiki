# Fishing Lures

> **Note:** You can craft Fishing Lures at the Marina table!

**Fishing Lures**

Fishing Lures can be crafted or found in Treasure Chests! They're an excellent way to improve both your chances of catching fish and the frequency of your catches.

Each lure is unique and designed for specific fishing rods. Be sure to check the item's tooltip to see which rod your lure is compatible with!

**Lure Effects:** Lures last for 30 minutes once activated and provide several benefits while active:

* **Faster Fishing:** Reduces the time between catch attempts, letting you fish more in less time
* **Better Catch Rate:** Increases your chance of successfully reeling in a fish
* **Rarer Catches:** Shifts the odds in your favor, making rare and valuable fish more likely to bite
* **Bonus XP:** Earn extra fishing experience with every catch

**Lure Tiers:**

| Lure          | Compatible Rods        | Overview                                                                                                                                       |
| ------------- | ---------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Basic Lure    | Basic Rod              | A modest boost to get you started, slightly faster catches and a small bump to your odds                                                       |
| Advanced Lure | Saltwater Rod, Fly Rod | A solid upgrade, noticeably faster fishing, better catch rates, and improved chances at uncommon fish                                          |
| Expert Lure   | Deepsea Rod            | The best lure available, significantly faster fishing, the highest catch rate bonus, and a major shift toward rare fish like Sharks and Whales |

**Tips:**

* Only one lure can be active at a time
* Lures stack with Hotspot bonuses! Fish in a Hotspot with a lure active for the best results
* You'll get a notification when your lure helps attract a catch, so you'll know it's working!
