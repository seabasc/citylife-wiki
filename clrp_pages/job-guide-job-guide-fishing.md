# Fishing

> **Note:** : Not all fish are legal!

## How do you start Fishing?

### To start Fishing, simply buy a Fishing Rod and Bait&#x20;

<details>

<summary>Where can I buy a Fishing Rod and Bait?!</summary>

Great question! purchase fishing equipment by visiting any of our Hardware Stores - easily spotted by a white wrench icon on the map. Purchase a Standard Fishing Rod to kick things off, as advanced rods require a higher fishing skill level. Don't forget to grab some bait while you're there. Happy fishing!

</details>

With your new fishing gear in hand, you're ready to explore the thrill of the catch! Go ahead and cast your line in any body of water that calls to you. Remember, any spot with water is a potential fishing hotspot. Embrace the adventure!

## Fished your heart out?

Now comes the rewarding part! It's time to turn your hard-earned catch into cash. Head on over to La Spada Fish Market to sell your fish!
