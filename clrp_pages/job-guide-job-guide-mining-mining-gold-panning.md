# Mining - Gold Panning

Once you are level 25 in mining, you can now Gold Pan! Woo!

1. Visit the NPC at the mineshaft entrance marked on your GPS and obtain a gold pan.
2. Once equipped, scout for that perfect gold-laden spot - usually a bubbling brook or a serene river.
3. Once you've found your 'Eldorado', bring out the pan and start sifting. Pray to the Gold Gods, do a rain dance, or sing a lucky tune - whatever floats your gold boat!

> **Note:** The river pictured below is a well-known gold hotspot. But hey, feel free to pan anywhere along its winding course - the world is your gold-filled oyster!
