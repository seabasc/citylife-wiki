# Mining

> **Note:** Proceed with caution! the mines are a dark and dangerous place!

## How do you start Mining?

## Make your way to the Mineshafts!

<figure><img src="https://1220491511-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2FTjoPqB53oR42XfwTK5MK%2Fuploads%2FnoE8r8Gq01qkI7IXyJGv%2Fimage.png?alt=media&amp;token=2e223317-8ce5-4082-aa12-332c05358929" alt=""><figcaption><p>POSTAL: 5002</p></figcaption></figure>

Enter the mineshaft and mine the available stone nodes. Bring sufficient food, water, and pickaxes.

## Mined to your hearts content? Time to crack, and smelt!

<figure><img src="https://1220491511-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2FTjoPqB53oR42XfwTK5MK%2Fuploads%2FuXOHftodTqc0HvHLpR2p%2Fimage%20(1).png?alt=media&amp;token=0c3109f9-1e70-4694-831b-21cf7bdf95fd" alt="" width="192"><figcaption><p>POSTAL: 9306</p></figcaption></figure>

Once you've reached the Smelting Factory, venture inside and make your way to the rear. The Factory houses both stone cracking and smelting stations. Check out the pictures below to find where you can unleash your inner smith and turn your stone into precious metal!

<figure><img src="https://1220491511-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2FTjoPqB53oR42XfwTK5MK%2Fuploads%2FqjmZZrh9hZXTbymH73HV%2Fuse.png?alt=media&amp;token=ea2d1672-b72a-46ab-bb6d-d99988af978d" alt=""><figcaption><p>Smelt materials inside the furnace, and crack stone using the cracking tool.</p></figcaption></figure>
