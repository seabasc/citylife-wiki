## How do you start Bee Keeping?

### To start Bee Keeping, head over to B on your GPS!&#x20;

<figure><img src="https://1220491511-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2FTjoPqB53oR42XfwTK5MK%2Fuploads%2Fl9JT3nTiMuHZwiNcncXu%2Fbeekeeping.png?alt=media&amp;token=b8ff9b62-1926-4ac2-ba74-e9b985a873aa" alt="" width="230"><figcaption><p>POSTAL CODE: 4001</p></figcaption></figure>

1. **Meet the Bee Keeper:** Buzz on over to the Bee Keeper located near postal 4001 at the B icon on your GPS! She's your go-to gal for all things bee-related.
2. **Purchase Your Supplies:** Whether you're a newbie or a seasoned beekeeper, she’s got you covered. Plus, she's always happy to buy your surplus supplies. It's a win-win!
3. **Place Your Bee Hives:** Set up your beehives in a sunny spot and don't forget to add a queen bee for that extra buzz. The queen bee is like the VIP of the hive, bringing all the best perks!
4. **The Waiting Game Begins:** Patience is a virtue, especially in beekeeping. Your hives will keep buzzing and producing honey and wax without any extra effort from you. Just sit back, relax, and maybe sip some tea while you wait.
5. **Reap the Benefits:** Once your hives are full of sweet, sticky goodness, you can sell the honey and wax back to the Bee Keeper (Read the tip below as well). She’s always eager to trade and reward your hard work with some sweet cash!

> **Note:** Sell your Bee Wax to Mechanic Shops and Bee Honey to Restaurant Owners!
