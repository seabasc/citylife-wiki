# Pizza Deliveries

Deliver hot, fresh pizzas around Los Santos for Pizza This!

> **Note:** Make sure to deliver your pizzas on-time for a better tip!

## How do you start Pizza Deliveries?

#### To start Pizza Deliveries, head over to Pizza This! in Downtown Vinewood!

<figure><img src="https://1220491511-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2FTjoPqB53oR42XfwTK5MK%2Fuploads%2FFFT9KUI7rO2P52146nIm%2Fpizza.png?alt=media&amp;token=44f004c0-06db-4cb2-b1ea-4149fb448845" alt="" width="293"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

1. **Meet the Pizza Chef:** Head to Pizza This! in Downtown Vinewood and talk to the line cook by the door. He'll set you up with deliveries!
2. **Pay Your Deposit**: You'll need to pay a $500 deposit for the Faggio scooter. Don't worry - you get it back when you return the scooter safely!
3. **Get Your Route:** You'll be assigned 5-10 random deliveries around Los Santos. Each customer's order will vary in size (1-7 pizzas).
4. **Grab the Pizzas**: Head back to Pizza This! after each delivery to restock your pizza boxes. Check your inventory to see how many you need!
5. **Deliver on Time:** You have 5 minutes to complete each delivery from the time you leave the shop. Late deliveries = smaller tips!
6. **Watch Your Order Size**: Customers will be angry if you don't bring the exact number of pizzas they ordered. Bring extras to make them happy!
7. **Return to Shop**: After completing all deliveries (or whenever you're done), return the Faggio to Pizza This! to collect your payment.

> **Note:** See the linked Pizza article for complete unlock details!
