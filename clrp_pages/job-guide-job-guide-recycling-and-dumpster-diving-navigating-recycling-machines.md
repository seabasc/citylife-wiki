# Navigating Recycling Machines

This is a subcategory of Recycling, the Recycler takes base items and converts them into raw materials! (WIP)

> CAUTION: The lower an items durability, the lower the yield!

1. **Head to the Recycling Center**:\
   Locate your nearest recycling center and make your way there.

<figure><img src="assets/wiki/cff0d0129c82d6b420af.png" alt="" width="197"><figcaption><p>POSTAL: 9241</p></figcaption></figure>

2. **Use Your Third Eye** *(LALT)*:\
   Once at the recycling machines, use your third eye (LALT) to interact with them.

<figure><img src="assets/wiki/838c314eac4adde23fbb.png" alt="" width="375"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

3. **Check Your Inventory**:\
   Any items in your inventory that can be broken down by the selected machine will be displayed.
4. **Machine Requirements**:
   * Each machine processes different types of items.
   * Some machines have specific level requirements.

> **Note:** See the linked Recycling article for unlock details!
