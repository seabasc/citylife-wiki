# Recycling & Dumpster Diving

This job is a bit different, and mainly focuses on materials and their conversion into something more!

> **CAUTION:** The Recycle Center is only open from 9:00 AM - 21:00 PM!

> **Note:** You can search virtually any trash can & dumpster in the city for Dumpster Diving!

## How do you start Recycling & Dumpster Diving?

### To start Recycling, head over to the Recycle Center blip on your GPS!&#x20;

<figure><img src="assets/wiki/cff0d0129c82d6b420af.png" alt=""><figcaption><p>POSTAL: 9241</p></figcaption></figure>

1. **Meet the Recycler**: the recycling activity begins at the Recycle Center. Chat with the NPC outside to sell any dumpster diving treasures you've collected or save them to fulfill your crafting dreams!
2. **Go On Duty**: Step inside the Recycle Center and let your third eye guide you to the desk. Use it to toggle on your recycling duty. Your green journey begins now!
3. **Search and Find**: Now that you're officially a recycling warrior, make your way inside towards the glowing object. Let the hunt begin!
4. **Dig Deep**: Once you find the glowing object, channel your third eye once again to thoroughly search it. Every piece counts!
5. **Drop It Off**: After a successful search, your third eye will guide you to a new highlighted object. This is where you drop off your discoveries!
6. **Reward Time**: Dropping off your items doesn't just declutter the environment - it rewards you with precious recycling materials!
7. **Trade and Prosper**: With your freshly earned materials in hand, it's time to trade! Visit the ped near the drop off zone and exchange your recycling materials for useful items.
8. **Clock Out**: Once you've done your part for the planet, head back to the desk. Toggle off your recycling duty, knowing you've made CityLife Roleplay a cleaner and greener place to live!

> **Note:** See the linked Dumpster Diving article for unlock details!
