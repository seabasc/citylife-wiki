# Taxi Driver

> **CAUTION**: A $300 deposit is needed!

## How do you start Taxi Driving?

### To start Taxi Driving, head over to the Taxi Driver blip on your GPS!

<figure><img src="assets/wiki/693f6eb343d2f280ff06.png" alt="" width="194"><figcaption><p>Postal 7295</p></figcaption></figure>

**Choose Your Path:**

* **Passenger Service:** Fancy yourself a people person? Offer rides to other players and turn every journey into a networking event on wheels.
* **Lone Cruiser:** Prefer the quiet hum of the road? Opt for NPC jobs and cruise the city at your own pace.

1. Press and hold 'Z' to whip out your Radial Menu and select "Taxi." while inside your Taxi.
2. Are you a people person? Start by showing off your fare meter to start charging for your charismatic chauffeur services once you’ve snagged a client. Start the meter, select a price, and go!
3. Or if you're a lone cruiser, toggle over to NPC Missions to hop into the queue for automated adventures.
4. When it’s your turn, you’ll get a location to scoop up your customer.
5. Whisk them away to their destination, deliver top-tier service, and then collect your well-earned payment automatically!
6. Done cruising? Toggle off NPC missions, return your trusty boss, and get your deposit back as you clock out.

> **Note:** See the linked Taxi article for unlock details!
