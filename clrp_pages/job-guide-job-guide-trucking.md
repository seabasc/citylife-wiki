# Trucking

> **CAUTION**: To rent a truck, a $500 deposit will be required!

## How do you start Trucking?

### To start, head over to one of the Trucking blips on your GPS!

<figure><img src="https://1220491511-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2FTjoPqB53oR42XfwTK5MK%2Fuploads%2FnnMb81SxUhQg2FhlcxaN%2Ftrucking.png?alt=media&amp;token=6fe03886-b6ee-462e-be14-6d46d8aedc52" alt="" width="212"><figcaption><p>There are two trucking bosses! <br>At the docks, and at Paleto!</p></figcaption></figure>

1. **Meet the Boss**: Start the trucking route . Your boss awaits you there. Rent your powerhouse of a truck for just $500 and get ready for an adventurous ride.
2. **Receive Your Truck**: The rented truck will arrive within 15 seconds.
3. **Connect the Trailer**: After you've entered your Truck, connect it to the Trailer by lining it up and pressing H!
4. **Embark on Your Route**:  Now you're off! Drive safely to  to your target location. Embrace the open road!
5. **Line It Up**: Once you've arrived, align your vehicle with the Ghost Truck's Projection. This is where the real work begins. It's unhitching time!&#x20;
6. **Unhitching Your Truck:** To Unhitch your truck, hop out, and use your third eye (L ALT) on your Truck to start the Unhitching process!
7. **Unload and deliver your goods**: Head to the back of your Trailer, and once again use your third eye (L ALT) to start unloading your cargo!
8. **Delivery Time**: Once you've unloaded the cargo, head to the client on your GPS and once again use your third eye (L ALT) to deliver the cargo to the buyer! Do this until you no longer have any goods left!
9. **Collect Your Pay**: You've not just transported items, you've mastered the art of logistics. Now, it's time to reap the rewards. Head back to the boss you started from, and third eye him (L ALT) to finish the job!

> **Note:** See the linked Trucking article for complete unlock details!
