# Cooking

Cooking is a skill that can be leveled by Cooking food! Below is your Cooking Skill cheat-sheet.

## Unlockable

* [x] Reaching Level 10 Unlocks Small Recipes
* [x] Reaching Level 20 Unlocks Medium Recipes
* [x] Reaching Level 30 Unlocks Large Recipes
