# Delivery (Post Ops)

Delivery is a skill that can be leveled by making deliveries for Postops! Below is your Delivery Skill cheat-sheet.

## Unlockable

* [x] Reaching Level 15 Unlocks Engine Level 1 Upgrades
* [x] Reaching Level 25 Unlocks Medium Delivery Routes, and Engine Level 2, Brakes Level 1 Upgrades
* [x] Reaching Level 35 Unlocks Brakes Level 2, Transmission Level 1 Upgrades
* [x] Reaching Level 45 Unlocks Engine Level 3, Brakes Level 3, Suspension Level 1 Upgrades
* [x] Reaching Level 50 Unlocks Large Delivery Routes
* [x] Reaching Level 60 Unlocks a Turbo, Suspension Level 2, and Transmission Level 2 Upgrades
* [x] Reaching Level 75 Unlocks a new and upgraded Delivery Vehicle, Engine Level 4, Transmission and Suspension Level 3!
* [x] Reaching Level 90 Unlocks Max Upgrades!

## **Reward Boosts**

* [x] As you level up, your lucky pay also increases. This scales infinitely with skill level!
* [x] Reaching Level 15 Boosts Your Special Delivery Chance!
* [x] Reaching Level 25 Boosts Your Special Delivery Chance!
* [x] Reaching Level 35 Boosts Your Special Delivery Chance!
* [x] Reaching Level 45 Boosts Your Special Delivery Chance!
* [x] Reaching Level 60 Boosts Your Special Delivery Chance!
* [x] Reaching Level 75 Boosts Your Special Delivery Chance!
* [x] Reaching Level 90 Boosts Your Special Delivery Chance!
