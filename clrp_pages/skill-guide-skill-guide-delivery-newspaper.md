# Newspaper

Newspaper is an easy-to-learn skill that levels up by simply delivering Newspapers! Below is your Newspaper skill cheat-sheet.

## Unlockable

* [x] Reaching Level 10 Increases the Base Payout by 3%
* [x] Reaching Level 15 Decreases the Job Cooldown by 5 Minutes!
* [x] Reaching Level 25 Increases the Base Payout by 5%
* [x] Reaching Level 30 Decreases the Job Cooldown by 10 Minutes!
* [x] Reaching Level 45 Decreases the Job Cooldown by 15 Minutes!
* [x] Reaching Level 50 Increases the Base Payout by 8%, and lets you Start Work Early, and End Work Late!
* [x] Reaching Level 75 Increases the Base Payout by 12%
* [x] Reaching Level 100 Increases the Base Payout by 15%!
