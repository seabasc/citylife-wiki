# Drug Sales

Drug sales is a skill that can be leveled by selling Drugs! Below is your Drug Sales Skill cheat-sheet.

## Unlockable

* [x] At Level 0, start with OG Kush Joint Sales, and Mushroom Sales
* [x] Reaching Level 10 Unlocks Mac 10 Joint Sales
* [x] Reaching Level 20 Unlocks Moonshine Sales
* [x] Reaching Level 25 Unlocks Madman OG Joint Sales
* [x] Reaching Level 30 Unlocks Meth Sales
* [x] Reaching Level 40 Unlocks Heroin Sales
* [x] Reaching Level 45 Unlocks Blue Dream Joint Sales
* [x] Reaching Level 60 Unlocks Candy Kush Joint Sales
* [x] Reaching Level 75 Unlocks Tropical Kush Joint Sales
