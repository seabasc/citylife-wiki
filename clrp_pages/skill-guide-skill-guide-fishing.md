# Fishing

Delivery is a skill that can be leveled by Fishing! Below is your Fishing Skill cheat-sheet.

## Unlockable

* [x] Reaching Level 25 Unlocks Salt Water Fishing (New Rod)
* [x] Reaching Level 25 Unlocks the Ability to Fish Treasure Chests (SoonTM)
* [x] Reaching Level 50 Unlocks Fly Fishing (New Rod)
* [x] Reaching Level 75 Unlocks Deep Sea Fishing (New Rod)

## Mini-Games

* [x] Reaching Level 35 significantly reduces chance to receive the mini-game for the Basic Rod
* [x] Reaching Level 60 significantly reduces chance to receive the mini-game for the Salt Water Rod
* [x] Reaching Level 85 significantly reduces chance to receive mini-game for the Fly Fishing Rod
* [x] Reaching Level 100 significantly reduces chance to receive mini-game for the Deep Sea Rod
