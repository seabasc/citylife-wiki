# Diving

Diving is a skill that can be leveled by you guessed it, Diving! Below is your Diving Skill cheat-sheet.

## Unlockable

* [x] Reaching Level 15 unlocks new rare items in your reward pool & the ability to find underwater Containers!
* [x] Reaching Level 25 unlocks new rare items in your reward pool & the ability to find underwater Treasure Chests!
* [x] Reaching Level 35 unlocks new rare items in your reward pool!
* [x] Reaching Level 50 unlocks new rare items in your reward pool, Enhanced Scuba Tanks & the ability to find underwater Ruins!
* [x] Reaching Level 75 unlocks new rare items in your reward pool!
* [x] Reaching Level 85 unlocks new rare items in your reward pool!

## Reward Boosts

* [x] Every level bracket increases your chances to receive existing rare loot as well.
* [x] Reaching Level 25 Increases your chance to find underwater Containers, Treasure Chests, and Ruins by 2%
* [x] Reaching Level 50 Increases your chance to find underwater Containers, Treasure Chests, and Ruins by 4%
* [x] Reaching Level 75 Increases your chance to find underwater Containers, Treasure Chests, and Ruins by 6%
