# Garbage

Garbage is a skill that can be leveled by you being a Garbage Worker! Below is your Garbage Skill cheat-sheet.

## Unlockable

* [x] Reaching Various different levels will also change the color of your Garbage Truck!
* [x] Reaching Level 25 unlocks medium routes!
* [x] Reaching Level 35 unlocks a new and upgraded Garbage Truck!
* [x] Reaching Level 50 unlocks large routes!
* [x] Reaching Level 65 unlocks a Turbo, and level 1 performance upgrades on your Garbage Truck!
* [x] Reaching Level 75 unlocks level 2 Performance upgrades on your Garbage Truck!
* [x] Reaching Level 85 unlocks level 3 Performance upgrades on your Garbage Truck!
* [x] Reaching Level 100 unlocks max Performance upgrades Garbage Truck!

## Reward Boosts

* [x] Reaching Level 70 Unlocks an Enhanced Material Ticket Pool

## Truck Color Unlocks

* [x] Reaching Level 10-19 will change your Garbage Truck into a Cream Color
* [x] Reaching Level 20-29 will change your Garbage Truck into a Beige / Light Brown Color
* [x] Reaching Level 30-39 will change your Garbage Truck into a Yellow Color
* [x] Reaching Level 40-49 will change your Garbage Truck into a Orange Color
* [x] Reaching Level 50-59 will change your Garbage Truck into a Bright Green Color
* [x] Reaching Level 60-69 will change your Garbage Truck into a Blue Color
* [x] Reaching Level 70-79 will change your Garbage Truck into a Red Color
* [x] Reaching Level 80-89 will change your Garbage Truck into a White Color
* [x] Reaching Level 90-99 will change your Garbage Truck into a Metallic Black Color
* [x] Reaching Level 100+ will change your Garbage Truck into a Gold Color
