# Trucking

Trucking is a skill that can be leveled by Trucking! Below is your Trucking Skill cheat-sheet.

## Unlockable

* [x] Reaching Level 10 unlocks the ability to purchase a new Truck!
* [x] Reaching Level 15 unlocks Food, Furniture, and Retail Routes!
* [x] Reaching Level 20 unlocks the ability to purchase a new Truck!
* [x] Reaching Level 30 unlocks Medical, Electronic, and Automotive Routes, along with the ability to purchase a new Truck!
* [x] Reaching Level 40 unlocks the ability to purchase a new Truck!
* [x] Reaching Level 50 unlocks Military, Industrial, and Chemical Routes, along with the ability to purchase a new Truck!
* [x] Reaching Level 70 unlocks the ability to purchase a new Truck!
* [x] Reaching Level 75 Hazmat, Specialized, and Government Routes!
* [x] Reaching Level 85 unlocks the ability to purchase a new Truck!

## Reward Boosts

* [x] Reaching Level 25 Boosts the chance to receive Trucking Reward Boxes
* [x] Reaching Level 50 Boosts the chance to receive Trucking Reward Boxes
* [x] Reaching Level 75 Boosts the chance to receive Trucking Reward Boxes
* [x] Reaching Level 100 Boosts the chance to receive Trucking Reward Boxes
