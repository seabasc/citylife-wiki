# Hunting

Hunting is a skill that can be leveled Hunting animals! Below is your Hunting Skill cheat-sheet.

## Unlockable

* [x] Reaching Level 5 Unlocks Hunting Coyotes
* [x] Reaching Level 10 Unlocks Hunting Boars in both Hunting Zones
* [x] Reaching Level 20 Unlocks Hunting Bob Cats
* [x] Reaching Level 25 Unlocks Hunting Owls on the Main Land, and Parrots, Hornbills, and Macaws On Cayo Perico
* [x] Reaching Level 30 Unlocks Hunting Bears
* [x] Reaching Level 40 Unlocks Hunting Brown Bears
* [x] Reaching Level 50 Unlocks Hunting Leopards at Cayo Perico
* [x] Reaching Level 65 Unlocks Hunting Mountain Lions in both Hunting Zones
* [x] Reaching Level 75 Unlocks Hunting Male Lions in both Hunting Zones

## Reward Boosts

* [x] Reaching Level 35 Boosts your chance to receive a wild rush!
* [x] Reaching Level 50 Boosts your chance to receive higher tier pelts, and boosts your chance to receive a wild rush!
* [x] Reaching Level 75 Boosts your chance to receive higher tier pelts, and boosts your chance to receive a wild rush!
* [x] Reaching Level 100 Boosts your chance to receive higher tier pelts!
