# Mining

Mining is a skill that can be leveled by you guessed it, Mining! Below is your Mining Skill cheat-sheet.

## Unlockable

* [x] Reaching Level 15 Unlocks Mining Tier 1 Asteroids
* [x] Reaching Level 25 Unlocks Gold Panning
* [x] Reaching Level 30 Unlocks Mining Tier 2 Asteroids
* [x] Reaching Level 35 Unlocks the Drill & Drill Bits
* [x] Reaching Level 60 Unlocks Mining Tier 3 Asteroids
* [x] Reaching Level 65 Unlocks the Laser Mining Tool

## Reward Boosts

* [x] Reaching Level 15 Reduces time taken to crack stone!
* [x] Reaching Level 35 Reduces time taken to crack stone!
* [x] Reaching Level 50 Reduces time taken to crack stone!
* [x] Reaching Level 75 Reduces time taken to crack stone!
* [x] Reaching Level 100 Reduces time taken to crack stone!

## Lucky Chance

* [x] As you level up, your lucky chance also increases, giving you a chance to receive more stone!
