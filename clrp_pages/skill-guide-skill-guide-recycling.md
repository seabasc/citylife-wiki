# Recycling

Recycling is an easy-to-learn skill that levels up by breaking down items and converting them into raw materials. Below is your Recycling skill cheat-sheet.

## Unlockable

* [x] Reaching Level 5 Unlocks New Recyclables!
* [x] Reaching Level 10 Unlocks New Recyclables!
* [x] Reaching Level 20 Unlocks New Recyclables!
* [x] Reaching Level 25 Unlocks New Recyclables!
* [x] Reaching Level 30 Unlocks New Recyclables!
* [x] Reaching Level 40 Unlocks New Recyclables!

## Reward Boosts

* [x] As you level up, your chance to get bonus materials while Recycling increases!
