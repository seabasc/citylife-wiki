# Smelting

Smelting is a skill that can be leveled by you cracking rocks, and smelting minerals! Below is your Smelting Skill cheat-sheet.

## Unlockable

* [x] Reaching Level 15 Allows you to smelt Iron Ore
* [x] Reaching Level 15 Allows you to smelt Aluminum
* [x] Reaching Level 25 Allows you to smelt Gold Nuggets
* [x] Reaching Level 35 Allows you to smelt Gold Ore
* [x] Reaching Level 40 Allows you to smelt Steel
* [x] Reaching Level 45 Allows you to smelt Uncut Emerald
* [x] Reaching Level 55 Allows you to smelt Uncut Citrine
* [x] Reaching Level 65 Allows you to smelt Uncut Sapphire
* [x] Reaching Level 65 Allows you to smelt Uncut Aqua Marine
* [x] Reaching Level 75 Allows you to smelt Uncut Diamonds and Rubies
* [x] Reaching Level 80 Allows you to smelt Golden Coins&#x20;
* [x] Reaching Level 85 Allows you to smelt Uncut Onyx & Celestial Cores
* [x] Reaching Level 90 Allows you to smelt Celestial Alloy

## Reward Boosts

* [x] Reaching Level 25 Reduces time taken smelt materials
* [x] Reaching Level 25 Increases chance to receive gems!
* [x] Reaching Level 35 Reduces time taken smelt materials
* [x] Reaching Level 35 Increases chance to receive gems!
* [x] Reaching Level 50 Reduces time taken smelt materials
* [x] Reaching Level 50 Increases chance to receive gems!
* [x] Reaching Level 75 Reduces time taken smelt materials
* [x] Reaching Level 75 Increases chance to receive gems!
* [x] Reaching Level 100 Reduces time taken smelt materials
* [x] Reaching Level 100 Increases chance to receive gems!
* [x] As you level up, your chance to get bonus materials while smelting increases!
