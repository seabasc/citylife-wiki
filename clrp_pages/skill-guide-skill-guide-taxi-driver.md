# Taxi Driver

Taxi Driving is a skill that can be leveled by you Driving a Taxi! Below is your Taxi Driver Skill cheat-sheet.

## Unlockable

* [x] Reaching Level 15 unlocks the Van Taxi
* [x] Reaching Level 25 unlocks the SUV Taxi
* [x] Reaching Level 40 unlocks the Limo Taxi
* [x] Reaching Level 50 reduces NPC mission queue times
* [x] Reaching Level 50 unlocks the Sports Taxi
* [x] Reaching Level 50 unlocks the Sports Taxi II
* [x] Reaching Level 65 unlocks the Sports Taxi II
* [x] Reaching Level 75 unlocks the Sprots Taxi III
* [x] Reaching Level 85 unlocks the Electric Taxi
* [x] Reaching Level 100 unlocks the Sports Taxi IV
