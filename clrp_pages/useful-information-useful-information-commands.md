# Commands

Common commands, clicking a command will provide a detailed description

<details>

<summary>/job</summary>

This command will tell you the job you are currently employed at, along with the position you hold there

</details>

<details>

<summary>/cash</summary>

This command displays the amount of cash on you

</details>

<details>

<summary>/bank</summary>

This command displays the amount of cash inside your bank

</details>

<details>

<summary>/e <em><code>[EMOTE]</code></em></summary>

/e c will cancel any emote\
\
This command will perform any emote you input\
\
**`EMOTE`:** Insert emote of your choice found in the emote menu

</details>

<details>

<summary>/skills</summary>

This command will display your various skills, and their level! You can also view any boosters you have active here.

</details>

<details>

<summary>/stats</summary>

This command will display your various Gym stats!

</details>

<details>

<summary>/report <em><code>[MESSAGE]</code></em></summary>

This command should be used to report another player if they are breaking the rules or engaging in behavior that is not tolerated. If you don't receive immediate help, please do not spam this command. For reports that require evidence or more details, please use our discord.

`MESSAGE`: This field will contain a detailed description of your report or issue at hand

</details>

<details>

<summary>/me <em><code>[MESSAGE]</code></em></summary>

This command will display a message above your head to interpret an action based on roleplay\
`MESSAGE`: This field will contain said action you are performing

</details>

<details>

<summary>/gang</summary>

This command will tell you your current gang, along with the position you hold there

</details>

<details>

<summary>/gangmenu</summary>

This command allows you to create, recruit, and manage your gang

</details>

<details>

<summary>/playtime</summary>

This command will tell you your current playtime in CityLife!

</details>

<details>

<summary>/vote</summary>

This command will open a link where you can vote for the city to get quick and easy rewards!

</details>

<details>

<summary>/ooctoggle</summary>

This command will toggle OOC feed from being seen, for that sweet peace of mind! \
\
**NOTE**: You will still see important messages!

</details>

<details>

<summary>/shuff</summary>

This command will change your characters seat inside of a vehicle

</details>

<details>

<summary>/seat <em><code>[0-5]</code></em></summary>

This command will change your characters seat inside of a vehicle according to the # you input. Example: /seat 0 is the drivers seat!

</details>

<details>

<summary>/removeprops</summary>

This command will remove any props stuck to your characters hands!

</details>

<details>

<summary>/carry</summary>

This command will carry the closest player

</details>

<details>

<summary>/boost</summary>

This command will reward you monthly in-game money if you're Discord Nitro boosting the CityLife Roleplay Discord!

</details>

<details>

<summary>/id</summary>

This command will display your characters temporary ID used for various things around the city. It's also referred to as "paypal" or "stateid"

</details>

<details>

<summary>/hud</summary>

This command will display your HUD settings. Silver supporters can customize this completely.

</details>

<details>

<summary>/scenes</summary>

This command opens your scene creator menu! CityLife Supports have access to create longer scenes, depending on their supporter level! Alternative commands: `/scene`, `/scenemenu`, (BINDABLE) `/openscenesmenu`

</details>

<details>

<summary>/guide</summary>

This command probably won't be useful as you're already here! But in-city, this command will open this guide for you!

</details>

<details>

<summary>/clear</summary>

This command is used to clear anything inside of your chat!

</details>

<details>

<summary>/oocg</summary>

To activate this feature, start by pressing 'T'. Next, tap the 'TAB' key. Lastly, type in '/oocg' followed by your message to share your thoughts in the global OOC chat! Do not spam or abuse this chat!

</details>

<details>

<summary>/oocl</summary>

To activate this feature, start by pressing 'T'. Next, tap the 'TAB' key. Lastly, type in '/oocl' followed by your message to share your thoughts in the local OOC chat! Do not spam or abuse this chat!

</details>

<details>

<summary>/prisonrep</summary>

This command is used to check your current prison gang, and your reputation level inside said gang.

</details>
