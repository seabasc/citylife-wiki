# Common Roleplay Terms

Common roleplay language used to help immerse yourself in the city

<details>

<summary>Eyes</summary>

Refers to your screen

</details>

<details>

<summary>Brain</summary>

Refers to your computer

</details>

<details>

<summary>Ears</summary>

Refers to your headset

</details>

<details>

<summary>Flying in or out</summary>

Refers to logging in and out of the server

</details>

<details>

<summary>Head Exploded</summary>

Refers to someones game / computer crashing

</details>

<details>

<summary>Pockets</summary>

Refers to your inventory

</details>

<details>

<summary>Tsunami</summary>

Refers to Server Restarts

</details>

<details>

<summary>Spider on you</summary>

Refers to your character potentially being bugged, and requires you to jump

</details>

<details>

<summary>In my head</summary>

Refers to you or another player being AFK

</details>

<details>

<summary>Think about <code>[COMMAND]</code></summary>

Refers to having to having to type out something in the chat

</details>

<details>

<summary>Voice box</summary>

Refers to your voice

</details>

<details>

<summary>Flex your <code>[KEY]</code> muscle</summary>

Refers to pressing a key on your keyboard

</details>

<details>

<summary>City</summary>

Refers to the server

</details>

<details>

<summary>Locals</summary>

Refers to NPC's around the city!

</details>

<details>

<summary>Alien</summary>

Refers to someone speaking "alien" which is speaking out of character

</details>

<details>

<summary>Chapter</summary>

Refers to ones skill level or experience. /skills

</details>
