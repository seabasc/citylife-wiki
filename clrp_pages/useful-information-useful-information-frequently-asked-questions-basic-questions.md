# Basic Questions

A short list with answers on how to do basic things around the city!

> **Note:** : Try to figure out the answers to your questions in character before asking in Discord. We will not always provide you with an answer. Please keep this in mind!

<details>

<summary>I'm New! Can you show me around?</summary>

Welcome to our community! While we'd love to personally guide every new member, it's not feasible due to time constraints. We encourage you to explore and learn in-character / in-game initially. Your proactive approach in seeking help here is a great first step. Enjoy discovering CityLife!

</details>

<details>

<summary>‍Whitelisted Jobs?!</summary>

You can apply for whitelisted jobs in our discord server in the <mark style="color:yellow;">#applications</mark> channel. It is recommended to have a week of playtime and a firm understanding of the city before applying! Good luck!

</details>

<details>

<summary>First Job</summary>

Work can be found all over the city! For more information on jobs, make sure to visit the [Job Guide](/job-guide/job-guide.md) section of our Wiki. Safe travels!

<details>

<summary>Help! I am Injured and need assistance</summary>

If you're injured and need assistance, you can check in at the various hospitals around the map. Pillbox is the main hospital inside the city.\
\
You can also buy bandages from many different stores, which can help alleviate that bleeding or pain.

<details>

<summary>Getting a Drivers License (Currently not needed!)</summary>

Trying to do something and it says you need a commercial license? No problem! Head over to the yellow steering wheel or the Driving School on your map. Once there, go up the stairs to do the exam. After successful completion of the driving exam, you must go back in the circle on-top of the stairs and complete the “Commercial Driving Test”. After successful completion of this, you will be able now to do delivery jobs, and purchase vehicles!

</details>

<details>

<summary>Help, I cannot find my car!</summary>

If a vehicle is lost, it remains unavailable until the next tsunami returns it from the impound lot. Take precautions to avoid losing the vehicle.

</details>

<details>

<summary>The police seized my car! Where did it go?!</summary>

Did the cops swipe your wheels? No worries!

 Keep an eye on your in-game inbox for an email with the scoop on when your ride will be back. Once the time's up, head to The Tow Lot and scoop it back from the desk near the Tow Boss.

<details>

<summary>Getting a Weapons License</summary>

Ready to pack some heat? Swing by the courthouse marked on your map, and buy a weapons license from a friendly staff member there when they are in-city. A word of caution though, they come at a steep price - for good reason!

</details>

<details>

<summary> How to put someone inside a vehicle / take them back out?!</summary>

Simply open your radial menu ('Z' key), choose 'cuff options' for the player, and select 'Put In.' To remove the player from the vehicle, press 'L ALT' to activate your third eye and select 'Take out of Vehicle.'"

</details>

<details>

<summary> My car keeps stalling or having issues, what's wrong?</summary>

If your having car troubles, worry not! Taking care of your vehicle is imperative for it's long-term health. Mechanics can fix issues with your vehicles - for a price!

</details>

<details>

<summary>How can I directly give another player items?</summary>

**Here's how you can share items with another player!**

1. Press *F2*, and Choose the quantity you want to give inside the box
2. Right-click the item and select *'Give.'*
3. Check the top right of your screen, and select the name of the other player
4. Navigate using your arrow keys and confirm with *'Enter.'*

</details>

<details>

<summary>Someone else's music is too loud! How do I turn it down!?</summary>

**Here's how you can lower the volume of your FiveM Chromium Process!**

1. Tab out of your game, and right click the windows speaker icon in the bottom right.
2. Click 'Open Volume Mixer'
3. Navigate to the 'FiveM Chromium subprocess'
4. Configure the volume to your taste. We recommend 25-35%!

</details>

<details>

<summary>Starting a Gang</summary>

Starting a gang is simple. Find a group of people, come up with a name and color (optional), assert your dominance, and set a name for yourself. Remember, you don't always have to resort to violence to do this! Creativity and good roleplay is always rewarded and encouraged!

</details>

<details>

<summary>My FiveM keeps crashing!</summary>

There may be a few factors causing this issue. Here are some possible solutions!\
Try to switch from Windowed/Borderless windowed/Fullscreen to one of the other modes, if that doesn’t fix it try to restart FiveM. If  you are still having issues, [clearing your cache](#clear-my-fivem-cache) is the best option. Finally, If this option didn’t work, fully uninstall, and re-install FiveM/GTA V.&#x20;

</details>
