# Where do I buy things?

A short list of useful items and where you can purchase them at

<details>

<summary> Phone</summary>

Mirrorpark's Digital Den is your go-to hub for affordable, top-notch phones! Our devices also pop up in selected stores across the city - but remember, convenience comes at a price!

</details>

<details>

<summary>Food</summary>

Food can be bought at relatively every shop in the city!\
**TIP:** Want food that lasts longer and fills you up more? Visit a locally owned restaurant!

</details>

<details>

<summary>Vehicles</summary>

Ready to hit the road in style? Your dream ride awaits you at one of these fantastic locations:

* **Luxury Autos**: For the high-rollers looking for extravagance!
* **PDM**: Where variety meets affordability.
* **Sanders**: Trucks and more for the adventurous souls.
* **Larry's RV's**: Your home away from home on wheels.
* **Mosley's Used Car Lot**: Fantastic deals on pre-loved vehicles.
* **All dealerships** have an "open" category that doesn't require a salesman!

For those seeking something extra special, \
\
Looking to let go of your vehicle for a little tax? Visit the vehicle sales on your GPS!

</details>

<details>

<summary>Repair Kits</summary>

**Having Car Troubles?**\
Meet our repair kits: your on-the-go solution for those pesky vehicle engine hiccups. Find them at the hardware store or just head towards the white wrench on your map!

</details>

<details>

<summary> Fishing Supplies</summary>

Looking to cast a line? Stock up on fishing supplies (bait and rod) at the hardware store. Just look for the friendly white wrench on your map!

</details>

<details>

<summary>Drugs</summary>

Spoiler Alert: No, you won't find drugs at our stores. For those ventures, you'll need to build connections within our lively city!

</details>

<details>

<summary> Weapons</summary>

Weapons can be purchased from the various ammu-nations around the city.\
**`NOTE:`** You will need a weapons license to purchase a firearm.\
\
Learn about how to get your very own weapons license in [Basic questions](/useful-information/useful-information/frequently-asked-questions/basic-questions.md)!

</details>

<details>

<summary> Polaroid Camera &#x26; Filters</summary>

Snap some memories in style with Polaroid Cameras & Filters! Available for purchase at the Digital Den in Mirrorpark.

</details>

<details>

<summary>FitBit</summary>

FitBit's can be purchased at hardware stores! They are essential if you're one who often forgets to eat and drink!

</details>

<details>

<summary>Parachute</summary>

Parachutes can be purchased from the Leisure Shop! The Leisure shop can be found towards the Zancudo River!

</details>
