# Motels

Motels are a great, and cheap alternative to housing! Our custom system comes with many unique features!

Time to find a place to crash for the night! Presently, we've got six fabulous motels at your service - check them out along with a small description below!

Upon your arrival, make a beeline for the welcoming Motel Manager NPC who is always ready to assist! From reserving cozy rooms to managing guest lists, even prolonging your stay - they've got it all covered. Enjoy the comfort and convenience of our delightful motels at CityLife!&#x20;
