# Phone

Help navigating your Phone!

> **Note:** You can purchase a phone from relatively any store around the map! (Digital Den sells it cheaper!)

<details>

<summary>How do you open your phone?</summary>

You can open your phone by pressing F1!

</details>

<details>

<summary>Wenmo Application</summary>

Say hello to your new best friend for transferring funds on the go!\
The Wenmo app is an easy and new way to transfer money to friends.\
If you'd like to deposit or withdraw money, visit an ATM or bank!

</details>

<details>

<summary>Transferring Vehicles</summary>

Ready to pass the keys of your beloved ride? Here's how! Whip out your phone, head to the vehicles app, and pick the vehicle you're transferring. Hit 'sell', punch in the lucky new owner's ID along with the agreed price, then sit tight! They'll either snap up the offer or decline - easy peasy!

</details>

<details>

<summary>Twitter Application</summary>

Time to be a social butterfly! The Twitter app lets you stay connected, informed, and entertained. Post your thoughts, respond to trending tweets, or amplify other's voices with a retweet. Use the platform in accordance with the applicable rules.

</details>

<details>

<summary>Group Application</summary>

Welcome to the world of camaraderie with the group app! Your first stop to assemble your own A-team, join other formidable groups, and complete jobs that scream "teamwork" in CityLife Roleplay. Bring your friends or make new ones, it's time to join forces!

</details>

<details>

<summary>Employment Application</summary>

Say hello to your personal HR department! The employment app is the handy-dandy tool that lets you manage your jobs like a pro. Keep track of the current roster, clock in and out of duty, and be in control of your professional life. Your career path just got smoother!

</details>

<details>

<summary>Ping Application</summary>

Want to share your location with a fellow friend or partner in crime? Then the Ping application is the answer you've been looking for! Just enter their ID, and select "SEND PING" to send them your location!

</details>

<details>

<summary>Gallery Application</summary>

Keen to know where all of those snappy photos you've taken are saved? Check out the Gallery App! Select photos to copy links, inspect them, etc! Picture management made easy! P.S: Press ESC to properly exit your Camera application!

</details>
