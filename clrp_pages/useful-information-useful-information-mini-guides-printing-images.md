# Printing Images

> **CAUTION:** Only <mark style="color:orange;">Bronze</mark>+ supporters can print images!

## How do you print an item with an image?

### To start, visit the Digital Den in Mirrorpark

<figure><img src="https://1220491511-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2FTjoPqB53oR42XfwTK5MK%2Fuploads%2FAdZX55nn0I9AzdLswXzP%2Fdig.png?alt=media&amp;token=582427d4-0293-4e97-826c-575ce522bd3e" alt=""><figcaption><p>POSTAL: 7344</p></figcaption></figure>

1. Unleash your inner artist at the Digital Den shop! Here's how:
2. **Stock up on Supplies**: Purchase Printer Ink and Blank Paper, your essentials for a printing spree!
3. **Get to the Printer**: Stride over to the printer and engage your third eye.
4. **Select an Image**: Spot a cool image on Discord? Right-click it, select 'Copy Link' and paste it into the printer dialogue.
5. **Set Your Quantity**: Decide on the number of copies you want, and hit 'Print'.
6. **Voila!**: Wait a brief 15 seconds, and behold your 'Printed Document' - your chosen image perfectly replicated!

### Ready to print your heart out? Off you go!
