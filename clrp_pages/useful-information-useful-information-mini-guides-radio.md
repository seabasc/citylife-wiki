# Radio

Radio Comms 101: Staying Connected

> TIP: The default bind to toggle radio voice is CAPS

1. **Get Your Gear**\
   Visit any hardware store in the city to purchase a **Radio**. It’s the key to keeping your team in sync, whether you’re cruising with friends or planning bigger operations.
2. **Activate the Radio**
   * **Right-click** on the Radio in your inventory.
   * Select **Use** to power it up and bring up the frequency interface.
3. **Choose Your Channel**
   * At the center of your screen, you’ll see a field where you can **type in a frequency**.
   * Enter a channel number (e.g., 101.1, 200.5, etc.) and then press **Enter** to confirm.
   * You’ll connect to that specific frequency right away—just like tuning into a private station.
4. **Adjusting Volume & Disconnecting**
   * You can easily **turn the volume up or down** to suit your surroundings. Look at the image below for help with this
   * To leave a frequency (or fully shut off the radio), follow the image below.
5. **Pro Tips**
   * **Share the Frequency**: If you’re teaming up, make sure everyone agrees on the same channel before heading out.
   * **Switch Often**: If you suspect eavesdroppers, change frequencies regularly for better security.
   * **Distance:** Distance between your friends matters! The farther away, the weaker the frequency

With the right frequency and a solid plan, your radio becomes the backbone of any organized effort—whether it’s coordinating a daring escape or just chatting with friends across town. Stay sharp, stay connected, and keep the airwaves buzzing!
