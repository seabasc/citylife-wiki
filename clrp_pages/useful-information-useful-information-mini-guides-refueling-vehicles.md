# Refueling Vehicles

Ready to juice up your ride and hit the road with a roar?

> **Note:** Make sure to fully turn your engine off **(G)** before refueling to prevent any accidents!

Find your way to any Gas Station on the map, and begin refueling.

<figure><img src="https://1220491511-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2FTjoPqB53oR42XfwTK5MK%2Fuploads%2F18IMbLsTeQABkMI3Tjjf%2Fimage.png?alt=media&amp;token=06710279-2528-402e-9a67-dc5283f5a0cc" alt="" width="159"><figcaption><p>Look for this on your map!</p></figcaption></figure>

Once there, cozy your car up to a gas tank, like they're old pals. Then, use your third eye (**LALT**), and select '*Refill Vehicle*', and select Regular Gas. Now It's fueling time!

Got that done? Great! Head over to your vehicle, and use your third eye to select 'Send bill" option.&#x20;

Now, Whip out your phone like a tech-savvy wizard and pay that bill. Once you've done that, use your third eye again on your vehicle and select 'Refuel Vehicle > Start Refueling'&#x20;

When refueling is complete, use the Third eye on the gas tank to return the hose before driving away.

> **Note:** You can also purchase a Jerry can to refuel on the go!
