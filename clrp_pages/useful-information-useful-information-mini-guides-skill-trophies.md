# Skill Trophies

The pinnacle companion for those with maximum skill level!

## How to craft and use Skill Trophies

### To start, visit Smelting Factory

<figure><img src="assets/wiki/66d15a959895e026464c.png" alt="" width="245"><figcaption><p>Postal 9306</p></figcaption></figure>

1. **Visit the Smelting Factory**\
   Head upstairs near the anvil to get started.
2. **Crafting Requirements**
   * You must have **level 20 Crafting** to craft a Skill Trophy.
   * Each trophy requires **level 100 in the associated skill** to equip.
3. **Equipping Your Trophy**\
   Once crafted, **simply use the trophy item** to equip it.
4. **Customize Your Trophy**\
   After equipping, type **`/trophy`** in chat to customize its look and effects!
